Logician studying the formal foundations of reasoning — how meaning arises from inference, how proofs structure thought, and what these foundations reveal about artificial intelligence.

His public writing on logic, mathematics, and artificial intelligence has appeared in Aeon, the Times Literary Supplement, Mathematics Today, and The Conversation. His 2025 essay “High School Algebra and the Limits of AI” received the Graham Hoare Prize from the Institute of Mathematics and its Applications.
